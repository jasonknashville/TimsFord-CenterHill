🏛️ Major Civic Development — Jail Relocation The current DeKalb County Jail sits at 100 Public Square — directly in Smithville's downtown core next to shops and civic spaces. As of June 2025, commissioners voted 10–2 to eliminate the downtown site and 9–3 to purchase a new 71.5-acre parcel on Smith Road. A 51-cent property tax increase already passed in 2024 funds the debt service. This is a meaningful upside catalyst: when the jail relocates and the Public Square site is redeveloped, downtown Smithville's character changes materially.

🎣 Bill Dance Signature Lake — Official State Recognition Tims Ford has been designated a Bill Dance Signature Lake — a TWRA/TDOT initiative bringing upgraded public boat ramps, new pier infrastructure, and state-backed tourism investment. Winchester City Park renovations were nearing completion in April 2026, including a new 60-foot-wide boat ramp. The city contributed $225,000 for restroom upgrades as part of the deal. Additionally, TVA's first Reservoir Land Management Plan revision in 25 years was released in 2026, affecting ~12% of TVA-managed shoreline — worth reviewing before purchasing any lakefront lot.
Winchester's Downtown Investment: The city has spent $13.5 million over 15 years revitalizing its historic courthouse square — now a certified Tennessee Main Street Community with $5M annual operating budget. A new 20-year land use plan (approved June 2024) and $15M wastewater force main (targeting 2026 completion) signal sustained commitment to managed growth.

Key Market Observations

Price Gap Narrowing
The TFL premium over CHL has compressed from 50% in 2020/2022 down to 20–28% in 2024–2025. CHL is appreciating faster on a percentage basis, suggesting it may represent the better value play for a buyer entering today.
CHL 2020→2022
Median price surged +59% in two years as COVID-era demand hit the lake market. DOM collapsed from 44 days to 5 days — an extraordinarily competitive seller's market that has since normalized.
TFL 2021→2022
Single largest year-over-year jump in the dataset: +46.9% median price gain, driven by Twin Creeks new construction volume and premium lakefront demand at the height of the pandemic migration.
Both Markets 2023–2024
DOM expanded and LTC stretched at both lakes simultaneously — a regional pattern consistent with elevated mortgage rates. Both markets are now buyer-friendlier than peak conditions.
TFL 2025 — New Record
Average sale price crossed $1,000,000 for the first time at Tims Ford in 2025. $/SF of $307 is an all-time high. Driven by Fanning Bend, Hopkins Point Bluff Dr, and Highland Ridge luxury inventory.
CHL 2025
Median pulled back slightly (-2.3%) to $627,500 but $/SF set a new record at $280 — suggesting price normalization at the volume-driven mid-tier while per-foot values remain strong.
Market Depth
TFL has roughly 2.5x the residential transaction volume of CHL. More liquidity on exit, but also a more crowded/competitive market to buy into. CHL buyers face less competition but a thinner resale pool.

For the Weekend / Family Use Case

Investment Analysis

Where your dollar goes farthest depends on budget tier. CHL dominates in the mid-range; TFL wins at the luxury end.

$500K – $750K CHL is the stronger play. Much more selection — Blue Water Bay, Hurricane Pointe mid-tier, The Falls at Center Hill. TFL at this range is mostly Twin Creeks townhomes and smaller homes without premium water access.
$750K – $1.2M Both lakes are competitive. CHL: Blue Water Bay upper tier, Harbor Pointe, Floating Mill Village. TFL: Barefoot Bay, Twin Creeks upper, entry-level Fanning Bend, Elklore, Cline Ridge. Decision comes down to drive tolerance and dock priority.
$1.2M+ TFL wins decisively in inventory quality and selection. Fanning Bend, Hopkins Point Bluff Dr, Highland Ridge, and Ridgeville Sub offer world-class lakefront at this tier with no comparable CHL offering.
📈
Appreciation Trajectory

CHL is the better percentage appreciator — +81% median over 6 years vs. TFL's +55%. TFL is the better absolute dollar appreciator starting from a higher base. Both markets normalized in 2023–2024; 2025 shows recovery.

💧
Market Liquidity

TFL has significantly higher transaction volume — roughly 2.5x as many residential closings per year. Easier to exit when needed. CHL has strong demand from the Nashville/Murfreesboro corridor but a thinner resale pool.

🏠
STR / Rental Potential

Both lakes are popular for short-term rentals. TFL's proximity to Jack Daniel's Distillery (Lynchburg, 20 min) and Tullahoma broadens the guest appeal to multiple metros — Nashville, Chattanooga, Huntsville. CHL's Nashville proximity (~1 hr) works for metro weekenders.

Notable Civic Developments to Track

Smithville / Center Hill
01 New Jail on Smith Road — Commission approved June 2025; 10–2 vote eliminated downtown site, 9–3 purchased 71.5-acre Smith Road parcel. Construction timeline TBD. When complete, Public Square site opens for potential redevelopment. Watch for rezoning proposals.
02 CDBG Street Paving Grant — City reapplying for $1M; redirected from water plant; outcome affects downtown road quality
03 TDOT Intersection Upgrade — Planned improvement at East Main & SR-56; minor but signals state infrastructure attention
04 New Smithville Elementary School — Under construction; first new school building in 67 years; funded without raising property taxes
05 Smithville CommUNITY Coalition Plan — 38.5-acre mixed-income housing master plan actively moving forward
06 Corps Shoreline Management Plan (2023) — Updated rules for dock permitting; ongoing enforcement of new guidelines
Winchester / Tims Ford
01 TVA Tims Ford Land Management Plan Revision — Public comment closed Aug 2025; new plan released ~March 2026. Affects ~12% of TVA-managed shoreline. Review before purchasing any lakefront lot.
02 Bill Dance Signature Lake Infrastructure — Winchester City Park boat ramp open; pier upgrades nearing completion April 2026; state-backed tourism investment ongoing
03 $15M Wastewater Force Main — New pump station near industrial park; targeting 2026 completion; enables residential and commercial growth
04 20-Year Land Use Plan (KCI) — Approved June 2024; formal roadmap for managed growth through 2044
05 Phase V Downtown Sidewalk Grant — Applied to TDOT for ~$1.1M; upgrades North College St near Oldham Theatre
06 Twin Creeks Marina Area — Continued commercial and residential development on eastern shore

Community Sentiment — Forums & Local Sources

Frequently called "underrated" and "underdiscovered" — locals feel it hasn't been priced fairly relative to Percy Priest or Old Hickory
Deep hills and coves give a more secluded, private feel than many comparable lakes — appreciated by buyers seeking quiet
The jail relocation vote was broadly welcomed; some residents felt the county should have built on the existing downtown site to save money on the new parcel
Growing developer interest in vacation condos is creating tension with preservation-minded locals who value the undeveloped shoreline character
Tims Ford / Winchester
Very positive overall — Winchester consistently cited as one of Middle Tennessee's most livable small towns; strong community pride
The boating community acknowledges peak summer can feel crowded, especially near marinas — some residents prefer the quieter northern coves
The Bill Dance Signature Lake designation was met with enthusiasm by local business owners, the fishing community, and the chamber
TVA's land plan revision has created some anxiety among existing shoreline permit holders — concern about potential reclassification of their access zones

Before Writing an Offer

Recommended Further Research

01 Verify HOA fees and STR restrictions in top target subdivisions — Twin Creeks, Fanning Bend, Blue Water Bay, and Hurricane Pointe all have HOAs. Short-term rental policies vary significantly and can materially affect rental income potential.
02 Review the new TVA Tims Ford Land Management Plan (2026) before purchasing any shoreline lot at Tims Ford. The plan was finalized in early 2026 and affects ~12% of TVA-managed land — potential reclassification risk for some parcels.
03 Confirm dock permitting status on any specific CHL property — Corps of Engineers requires pre-purchase permit verification. Grandfathered dock rights vs. new permit requirements are property-specific and non-transferable in some cases.
04 Track Smithville jail relocation timeline — Construction start date TBD. The downtown redevelopment opportunity it creates could serve as a price catalyst for CHL properties near the Public Square. Monitor rezoning and RFP activity from DeKalb County.
05 Pull and compare property tax bills — DeKalb County (CHL) current rate is $2.51/$100 assessed value (recently increased). Franklin County (TFL) rate should be confirmed as it has been rising with rapid infrastructure investment and development activity.
